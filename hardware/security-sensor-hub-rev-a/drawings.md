# Critical dimensions and fabrication notes

- Enclosure outside: 220 × 170 × 72 mm
- Wall thickness: 3 mm ASA/PETG baseline; use an off-the-shelf rated enclosure for outdoor/regulated sites
- Lid overlap: 10 mm; M4 heat-set inserts at four corners
- DIN/electronics plate: 190 × 140 × 2 mm aluminium or 3 mm FR4/printed plate
- Cable entry zone: lower 40 mm of the long side; drill glands to the actual cable schedule
- Speaker is normally external; only a grille is provided for a small indoor demonstrator speaker
- Keep ADC/field terminals at least 30 mm from amplifier and output wiring

The CAD is a supplier-neutral enclosure/layout source. Terminal blocks and boards must be measured before cutting final mounting holes.
