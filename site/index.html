What “complete” means here

Software, simulation, firmware interfaces, parametric CAD, BOMs, wiring guidance and Rev-A engineering specifications are complete enough for a digital prototype release. Fabrication, field calibration, endurance testing and certification remain a future physical-validation phase.

Safety boundary

OpenPatrol creates incident candidates; it does not guarantee detection. Certified fire, pool, medical and access systems remain independently operational.
